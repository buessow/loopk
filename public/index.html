Loop misses

Lows below 3.9 mmol/L (70 mg/dL) after SMBs in the hour before that the low leaves no room for, and highs above 13.9 (250) the loop dosed for far too mildly or was capped on. The amounts are estimates from the loop's ISF, for finding patterns, not exact doses. Pick one to show it on the chart.

    Press "Select time range", then drag across the chart to select up to 12 hours (with a mouse, shift-drag selects at any time). Claude gets the readings, treatments and loop runs from 6 hours before to 3 hours after the selection, the profile, and a guide to how AAPS doses, and judges whether the loop over- or under-corrected, and why.

      Basal lane

      The dashed step line is the basal profile: the hourly rates programmed in the pump. The filled steps are the basal actually delivered. Where the fill rises above the dashed line, the loop raised the rate with a temporary basal; where it drops below it or to zero, delivery was reduced or suspended.

      Carbs on board

      The COB line is the loop’s estimate of absorbable carbohydrate still active in the body. It is reported with each device-status update, so breaks in the line indicate missing loop-status data rather than zero carbs.

      Insulin on board

      The IOB line in the same lane is the loop’s insulin still active, in units, on its own scale (values on the left; on phones, on the right). It can go below zero when the loop delivered less than the profile basal.

      Events and glucose

      Boluses (triangles, units) and carbs (circles, grams) sit on the same time axis as the readings, with a guide line down into the glucose lane. Glucose is green inside the 3.9–10 mmol/L target band, red above it and blue below it.
